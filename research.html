--- title: Research --- Research

I study how plant reproductive systems evolve and what they mean for the populations that carry them. Working mainly with bromeliads, I pair phylogenomics, which reveals patterns of reproductive change across a genus, with population genomics, which shows how those systems shape mating and genetic diversity within species.

Catopsis

Catopsis Griseb. is an epiphytic genus in the bromeliad subfamily Tillandsioideae. Its greatest diversity lies in Mexico, though species range across the Caribbean, Central America, and the Brazilian Shield of South America.

Remarkably, Catopsis is one of the few bromeliad lineages with dioecious species,a rarity within the family. Elsewhere, dioecy is documented only in Hechtia (Hechtioideae), the monotypic Androlepis skinneri, and Aechmea mariae-reginae (both Bromelioideae).

Both Hechtia and Catopsis share Mexico as their diversity center, while the two Bromelioideae species with a dioecious sexual system are found in Central America.
